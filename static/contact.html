How may we help?

Most questions are answered below. If yours is not, email or call us, or use the enquiry form. We reply within two working days.

Common questions

How do I book a course?

Log in to My Academy, choose the course and pay by card. If your employer is paying, choose "Invoice" at checkout and add a purchase order number.

When do I get my exam result?

Results for online exams appear in My Academy within ten working days. Case-study and portfolio results take up to four weeks.

Can I change my exam date?

Yes, free of charge up to seven days before your slot. After that, a £25 change fee applies unless you are ill or have an emergency.

I have forgotten my password.

Use "Forgot password?" on the login page. If the email does not arrive within an hour, contact Member Services.
